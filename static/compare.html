About the Models
Isolation Forest tree-based

Randomly partitions the feature space using binary trees. Anomalies are isolated in fewer splits because they sit far from the bulk of normal data. Fast and scalable, but the fixed contamination threshold makes it conservative — high precision, lower recall.

Used as the live stream model. Scores here are normalised to 0–100 based on training-data percentiles.

Local Outlier Factor density-based

Compares the local density of a point to its k nearest neighbours. A point in a sparse region surrounded by dense neighbours scores high. Catches local anomalies that global methods miss, often achieving the best F1 on this dataset.

Sensitive to the n_neighbors hyperparameter (here: 20).

One-Class SVM boundary-based

Learns a tight RBF-kernel hypersphere around the normal training data. Points outside the boundary are flagged. Good when the normal region is compact, but the kernel boundary can be irregular — leading to more false positives on noisy sensor data.

Raw scores (signed distance to boundary) are rescaled to 0–100 for this comparison.

Elliptic Envelope statistical

Fits a multivariate Gaussian to the training data and flags points with a Mahalanobis distance above the contamination threshold. Works well when sensors are linearly correlated (pressure–temperature coupling fits this). Breaks down if the distribution is non-Gaussian.

Raw Mahalanobis distances span enormous ranges; scores are normalised before display.

Z-Score Baseline statistical baseline

Takes the maximum absolute z-score across all standardised features. Flags readings where any single sensor deviates by more than 3.2 σ from its training mean. Fully interpretable — a reading is flagged only when one sensor clearly breaks its own range.

Misses multi-feature anomalies where no single sensor exceeds the threshold alone.

Why do scores differ run to run? Each simulation run uses a new random seed, so anomaly timing and types vary. The pre-generated sample dataset (100 cycles) gives reproducible results for direct comparison.

Why is Isolation Forest the live model if LOF scores better here? Isolation Forest runs in O(1) per reading after training — it can predict in microseconds. LOF with novelty=True is slower per prediction and less stable at very high streaming rates. For batch analysis (this page), LOF is the better choice.
